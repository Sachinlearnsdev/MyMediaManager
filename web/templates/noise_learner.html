Noise Learner

Experimental Feature

The noise learner automatically identifies potential noise words from filenames by comparing raw names to API-confirmed titles. It can make mistakes — a word flagged as noise might be a legitimate part of another title. Patterns are always learned in the background, but only applied to filename cleaning when you enable it below. Review and approve patterns before enabling.

Apply Learned Patterns

When enabled, learned noise patterns are stripped from filenames during processing. When disabled, the system still learns but does not apply patterns.
